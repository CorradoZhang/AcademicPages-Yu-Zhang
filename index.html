About Me

I am a PhD student in Computer Science at the University of Galway, working in the A3 Research Group under the supervision of Dr. Karl Mason.

Before beginning my doctoral research, I completed an MSc in (Computer Science) Artificial Intelligence at the University of Galway from 2024 to 2025, and dual undergraduate degrees, a BEng in Computer Science and Technology and a BSc in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics, at Heilongjiang University of Science and Technology from 2020 to 2024.

My research focuses on reinforcement learning, optimization, and decision-making under uncertainty, with applications to robotics and autonomous systems. I am interested in developing adaptive and reliable methods for complex, real-world environments.

I welcome collaboration on problems related to machine learning, control, and intelligent systems.

Research Interests

  • Reinforcement learning
  • Robotics
  • Decision-making
